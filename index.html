Programs

Three ways to bring customers back.

Start with one or run all three. Each is a branded pass your customers keep in their wallet.

Loyalty & stamp cards

Digital punch cards and points that track themselves.

  • Buy-9-get-1-free, or points
  • Updates instantly at the counter
  • Never lost, never reprinted

Coupons & promos

Redeemable offers delivered straight to the wallet.

  • Percent-off, BOGO, first-visit
  • Set an expiry date
  • Push to everyone who holds it

Membership & store cards

VIP tiers, gym access, store credit — always current.

  • Tiers, status & member IDs
  • QR / barcode scan-in
  • Message members directly
The difference

The punch card, reinvented.

Everything a paper card can't do — because the wallet is already open on every phone.

Paper punch card

  • Left at home or lost in a drawer
  • Reprint and restock forever
  • No way to reach a customer again
  • Zero data on who's coming back

Repass wallet pass

  • Always in the phone, next to boarding passes
  • Nothing to print — ever
  • Push an offer to every holder in seconds
  • See visits, redemptions & repeat rate
How it works

Live in about a week — you do almost nothing.

We handle the Apple Developer account, the certificates, the design, and the software behind it.

1

Share your brand

Send your logo, colors, and what the card rewards. We design a pass that matches your shop.

2

We deploy it

We build the program on our infrastructure and set up your sign-up page and staff scanner.

3

Customers join

They tap an NFC card or scan a QR at the counter and the pass drops into their wallet.

4

It runs itself

Staff scan to add points or redeem. Passes update instantly, and you push offers anytime.
